About

Hello! I'm Ben Gutierrez. I studied electrical engineering with a physics minor at San Diego State University. I have a master's in applied physics from Caltech, where I'm now a PhD candidate.

My research is on integrated photonics — frequency comb sources in thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN). This site is where I keep that work, along with the art and a few older projects.
