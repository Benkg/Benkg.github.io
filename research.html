Research

Mode-locked lasers and frequency combs in thin-film lithium niobate, from device design through layout and simulation.

I am a PhD candidate at Caltech in the nonlinear photonics laboratory. My primary work is on mode-locked laser sources and frequency combs in thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN). Most of that is design, layout, and simulation of nanophotonic circuits — how the laser, modulator, and nonlinear waveguide sit together on a chip, and how those choices show up in the pulse and the comb.

Undergraduate work was in electrical engineering with a physics minor, largely on diffractive optics: high phase-delay spatial light modulators and the extra degree of freedom they give a phase mask.
